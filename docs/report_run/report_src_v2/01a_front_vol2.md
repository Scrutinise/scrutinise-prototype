# How to read this volume

## How to read this volume

**This volume works all twelve measures against the statute book.** Volume 1 sets out what David's Programme is, the argument it turns on, what David said and what the law would have to do, and the limits on the whole analysis. Volume 3 carries the supporting schedules.

**Each measure is worked to the same template**, so that any two can be compared directly.

**The strategic kernel** — the core logic of the measure, and the only part of it that is a strategy:

- **Diagnosis** — the problem, its causes, and why it persists
- **Guiding policy** — the approach proposed
- **Coherent actions** — what would actually be done, and by whom

**Decisions, challenges and research** — everything that sits outside the kernel:

- **Consequential decisions** — questions this measure raises that cannot be settled inside it
- **Challenges to the proposal** — each one a question the proposal has not yet answered
- **The critique revisions** — adversarial review by an alternative AI model, and what it changed
- **Difficulties caused by the law today** — the provisions themselves, quoted from the statute
- **What the courts have said** — the judgments retrieved
- **Evidence that runs the other way** — material that cuts against the measure, kept rather than dropped
- **The evidence base** — what was retrieved and read

**Three measures are worked in full in Part 6** — the Human Rights Act, the Equality Act and the civil service. **The other nine are worked in summary in Part 7**, the last of them being the Great Repeal itself. Whether the twelve have to move together is tested separately, in Part 3 of Volume 1.

⚠ **Every count in this volume is a count of what was retrieved and read, not of what exists.** Where a figure is given for provisions, judgments or instruments, it means that many were found and examined. No count is presented as complete. The limits are set out in full in Part 5 of Volume 1.
