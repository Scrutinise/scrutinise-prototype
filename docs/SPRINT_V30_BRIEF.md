## 0. GOVERNING POLICY — sensitive evidence (read first; applies to §3)
Charlie's decision (24 Jun, after a data-protection discussion): ingest inquiry/review **evidence**, but **exclude clearly-sensitive personal categories** — survivor/victim personal testimony involving abuse, individual medical-record/health-detail statements, and statements given under anonymity or restriction orders. **Rationale (record in `SENSITIVE_EVIDENCE_POLICY.md`):** "public" ≠ exempt from UK GDPR — bulk re-indexing of special-category data is a fresh accountable processing act; inquiries publish such material under restriction/anonymisation regimes that bulk ingestion can breach; and the legislative-analysis value of personal testimony is low while the harm potential (to identifiable, already-harmed people) and reputational risk (to a civic-trust platform) is high. **Implementation must be EASY, not fine-grained:** exclude at the level the inquiry's OWN structure already separates — a labelled testimony category, a restriction-marked bundle, an anonymised-statements section. Do NOT attempt per-paragraph PII redaction. Where a bundle mixes sensitive + valuable material and is NOT cleanly separable, **flag it in the report with a recommendation — do not ingest blind and do not drop wholesale.** Keep: final reports, expert reports, official/government/institutional evidence, public-hearing transcripts, statistical/factual exhibits.
