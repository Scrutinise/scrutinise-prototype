# BRIEF INGEST-CBA-1 — what Lex needs from the corpus for cost–benefit analysis

**From CCh-Lex to CCh-Ingest, 4 November 2026.** Decision 314: costs are held on each action (cost lines) and analysed on the idea. The analysis will follow HM Treasury's Green Book shape. Lex can only do that from held, section-addressable text and from a small number of official tables. This brief says what, why, and in what form. Nothing here changes Lex; it is the material Lex's cost–benefit brief will be written against.

**Rule for everything below:** section-addressable (one row per numbered section or heading, with the document's own numbering kept), full text not a landing page, licence recorded, the publisher's own date, and a refresh cadence stated. Where a document is a PDF with numbered paragraphs, the paragraph number is the section key.

## 3. Precedent costings — what comparable measures cost

- The **Explanatory Notes and Impact Assessments** for the five precedent Acts (Building Safety Act 2022; FSMA 2000 as amended 2013; Corporate Manslaughter and Corporate Homicide Act 2007; Local Government Act 1972 s.151 / LGHA 1989 ss.4–5; Health and Care Act 2022): their "financial effects" sections and IA cost tables. Explanatory Notes are in the corpus for some Acts (CRaG's are [Ref: 25]); the IAs are the gap.
- **Public Accounts Committee and NAO** reports on SRO turnover, project delivery, accounting officer failures: the cost figures as §2 row 6.

## 4. What Lex will do with it (so the form fits)

- Each action's **costing companion** (26-V2b kind "Costing") lists cost lines by category (one-off / recurring, who pays, basis, price year) and cites the unit cost or benchmark used (`[Ref: n]` to a section or a table row).
- The idea's **cost–benefit analysis** rolls the lines up by heading and by year, converts to one price year with the deflators, discounts at the Green Book rate, applies optimism bias by category, states benefits where a valued outcome exists and "not valued" where none does, and prints sensitivity. Every number traces to a line, a table row or a held section.
- The **Impact Assessment** output (a later document kind) mirrors the IA template's fields so a reader from a department sees a familiar shape.
