## §1 — Finishing the kernel is an event, and the product must mark it

**When all four kernel sections are settled, say so and say what is now possible.** At present the
flow simply ends.

**1a.** ⚠ **The first thing offered is the coherence check.** Charlie's decision 87b said it runs on
the first draft; **report whether it has ever run**, and if it has not, say so plainly before
offering it as a button.

**1b. The second is an honest account of what is still open**, drawn from real counts:
- **challenges not yet answered** — 176 on his idea;
- **decisions still unresolved**;
- **things still to read.**

⚠ **These already live at the top of the left panel and he never saw them.** Naming the numbers at
the moment the kernel closes is the fix.

**1c. A re-run is likely to be wanted** now the kernel is settled — ⚠ **but say why, not just that.**
A re-run after the kernel is complete searches against decisions that did not exist when the last one
ran. **That sentence is the reason, and the user should see it.**

## §2 — The box that currently offers only a rebuild

**It becomes the place the user goes at the end, offering a range of options, each with a line saying
why you would choose it.** Charlie: *"Make that one of a range of options with guidance on why I
would choose it or other options."*

**At minimum:**
- **Run the coherence check** — does the policy answer the diagnosis, and do the actions implement
  the policy?
- **Answer the outstanding challenges** — N unanswered; these are the objections your proposal will
  meet.
- **Re-run** — searches afresh against everything you have settled since the last build.
- **Take the working document away** — §3.
- **Go deeper** — §4, ⚠ **shown only if and when it does something.**

⚠ **Anything not yet built says so, in the four-state form already agreed.** An option that looks
available and does nothing is worse than one that admits it is coming.

## §3 — The working document

**A single document the user can take away at the end of the kernel, containing:**
- **the kernel** as settled — diagnosis, guiding policy, coherent actions;
- **the challenges**, answered and unanswered, distinguished;
- **what there is still to read.**

⚠ **Report first whether this already exists.** The evidence pack, the proposal, First Scrutiny and
Initial Questions all exist; **a fifth document nobody asked for is worse than signposting one of
those four.** If one already carries most of this, **say so and propose extending it.**
